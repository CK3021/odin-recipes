Homemade Lasagna

A classic homemade lasagna layered with seasoned ground beef,
rich tomato sauce, ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375 F.
  2. Cook lasagna noodles.
  3. Brown ground beef and add tomato sauce.
  4. Mix ricotta, egg, and Parmesan.
  5. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheese.
  6. Repeat the layers.
  7. Bake for 35-45 minutes.
  8. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
