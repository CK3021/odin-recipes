Honey Glazed Chicken

Juicy, pan-seared chicken coated in a sweet and savory honey garlic glaze.
Made with fresh garlic, butter, soy sauce, and honey for a flavorful homemade dinner.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken breasts
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Cook chicken until golden brown and cooked through.
  4. Remove chicken from the skillet.
  5. Add butter and garlic to the skillet.
  6. Stir in honey, soy sauce, and lemon juice.
  7. Simmer until the glaze thickens.
  8. Return chicken to the skillet.
  9. Coat chicken with the honey garlic glaze.
  10. Garnish with parsley and serve.
